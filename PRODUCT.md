# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site deployed through GitHub Pages.

## Users

Primary visitors are recruiters, CEOs, CTOs, finance professionals, and people
who want to understand Maria Gabriela Marquina's professional profile and
career. They are checking whether her billing, collections, or operations
background fits a remote or Buenos Aires role.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Maria Gabriela Marquina as an administrative and operations
professional: experience, skills, career path, and how to reach her. Success
means a recruiter or hiring manager emails her or opens her LinkedIn.

## Positioning

Maria's profile connects billing, accounts receivable, reconciliations,
commercial operations, client care, document control, and continued Psychology
studies. The copy should name specific systems, employers, and duties, and
show she works with both numbers and people.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive while screening candidates, evaluating remote support for
their business, or learning more about Maria through a referral. The homepage
should state what she does in the first screenful, before anyone opens the CV.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Deliver the core profile in Spanish and English, with Spanish as the primary
  public audience language.
- Provide direct public contact actions for email and LinkedIn.
- Provide downloadable Spanish and English CV/resume versions.
- CV/resume downloads must remain ATS-compatible and follow the agreed Harvard
  standards.
- Use static delivery suitable for GitHub Pages.
- Present three visual palettes: Indigo, Blush, and Elite.
- Present three avatar treatments: Normal, Anime, and Realistic.
- Preserve one professional narrative across all visual combinations.
- Exclude private journals, messages, personal galleries, relationship records,
  and unrelated private material.
- Maintain responsive behavior, keyboard usability, readable contrast, and
  meaningful image alternatives.

## Brand Commitments

- The public identity is María Gabriela Marquina Graterol.
- The tone is credible, warm, and direct.
- The visual system must support the 3 x 3 palette/avatar matrix.
- Existing avatar assets in `public/avatars/` are approved source assets.
- The site must present Maria as studying Psychology at UBA and open to
  remote or Buenos Aires roles in operations, billing, and client care.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved bilingual professional CV records (Spanish and English).
- Approved professional LinkedIn recommendation source.
- Approved remote-work positioning notes used only for professional framing.
- Nine approved avatar assets maintained as local portrait artwork for the
  public site (`public/avatars/` for delivery; source artwork kept outside
  the published content model).

No invented testimonials, business outcomes, performance metrics, or
third-party endorsements may be added without an approved source.
Do not embed absolute private-vault filesystem paths or private chat-export
references in active project documentation.

## Product Principles

- State what Maria does on the first screen.
- Lead with employers, systems, and duties that have a source.
- Treat the Psychology degree and career moves as facts on the timeline.
- Put email and LinkedIn within one click of the hero.
- Keep Spanish/English meaning aligned, privacy boundaries intact, and CV
  pages readable by ATS parsers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states,
readable contrast across all nine visual combinations, responsive layouts,
reduced-motion preferences, semantic structure, localized language metadata,
and meaningful alternatives for avatar imagery.
